For broadcasters & rights holders

Melodia uses a community-maintained station directory. A listener’s browser connects directly to the selected station’s stream. Melodia does not host or relay the audio. A listing does not mean that the station has approved Melodia.

Request a correction or removal

To report a station, email evangelion.conquest@gmail.com. Please include:

  • The station name and its Melodia link (including the station ID, if available).
  • The official station website or stream address and what should be corrected or removed.
  • How we can verify your connection to the station or rights at issue. Please do not send sensitive documents unless requested.

The email link opens your email app. Melodia does not send the message for you; please send it from there.

We review reports and can remove a station from Melodia’s catalogue. Removal from Melodia does not remove it from Radio Browser.
